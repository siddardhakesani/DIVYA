=== HOW TO ADD YOUR VIDEO MEMORIES ===

1. Export or save your favorite video clip or montage of you and Divya (from Instagram reels, phone camera, etc.).
2. Save it as an MP4 file.
3. Rename the file to:
   memory-video.mp4
4. Place it right here in this `assets/videos/` folder!

The video player in index.html is already pre-configured to play `assets/videos/memory-video.mp4`.
If the video file is not yet present, a romantic video preview card with memory message is displayed gracefully.
