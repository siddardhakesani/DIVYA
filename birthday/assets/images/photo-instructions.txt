=== HOW TO ADD YOUR COUPLE PHOTOS ===

1. Pick 6 of your favorite photos of you and Divya.
2. Rename them to:
   - photo1.jpg (e.g. The first day / memorable start)
   - photo2.jpg (e.g. Her radiant smile)
   - photo3.jpg (e.g. Your favorite coffee date / hangout)
   - photo4.jpg (e.g. A trip or stargazing moment)
   - photo5.jpg (e.g. A funny / goofy candid laugh)
   - photo6.jpg (e.g. Your favorite picture together / forever promise)

3. Place them right here in this `assets/images/` directory!

The website has smart fallback built-in: it will load `photo1.jpg`, `photo2.jpg`, etc., and if a file hasn't been uploaded yet, it gracefully displays the high-res romantic illustrated SVGs (`photo1.svg` - `photo6.svg`)!
